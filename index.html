Privacy Policy Halal Food Scanner App

Your privacy is protected by design

This Privacy Policy explains how Beyond7thSky ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Halal Food Scanner App. Our goal is to help you quickly verify ingredients while keeping your personal data safe.

Halal Food Scanner App is designed to help users check food ingredients, identify halal, haram, and doubtful items, and scan products using barcode/QR code scanning. We respect your privacy and do not collect unnecessary data.

2.1 No Personal Data Collection

The app does NOT collect, store, or share any personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or media files

2.2 Camera Permission

The app uses your device camera only for barcode/QR code scanning. We do not record, save, or upload any images or videos.

2.3 Internet Connection

Internet is used only for:

  • Displaying ads (Google AdMob or similar services)
  • Fetching updated ingredients data (if required)

Because we do not collect personal data, no information is stored or shared. Camera access is used strictly for scanning purposes.

The app may use third-party services that collect anonymous data for analytics and advertising, including:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob (for ads)

These services may collect:

  • Device information
  • App usage statistics
  • Ad performance data

This data is not personally identifiable and is handled in accordance with each provider's separate privacy policy.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The app is safe for all age groups.

Since we do not collect personal data, no sensitive information is stored. All data used in scanning is processed directly on the device.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. Continued use of the app after updates means you accept the revised policy.
